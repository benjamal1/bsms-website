Mentorship

Connect with e-board members for one-on-one mentorship meetings.

While BSMS may not be able to provide research or shadowing opportunities directly, we want to teach you how to find these opportunities yourself. Our members — including those with space in their labs — are always willing to connect people to opportunities as they arise, and through our guest speakers we're growing a network that opens doors for future opportunities. So while we can't do shadowing as a club, you can absolutely find shadowing through this club.
