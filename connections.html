Connections

We strive to create meaningful connections and conversations. The more involved you are, the more doors open — here's how.

Research Involvement

BSMS members are actively involved in orthopedic and sports medicine research. Rather than mass sign-ups, interested members apply to join specific ongoing projects or new project ideas — applications weigh interest, not experience. Members who put in the work gain authorship opportunities, invitations to research group meetings, and connections that carry forward over time.
