What We Do

The Brown Sports Medicine Society (BSMS) brings together students interested in sports medicine through guest speakers, hands-on workshops, and community-building events. We're a professional yet welcoming space, open to all students.

We regularly host guest speakers from sports medicine, surgery, and orthopedics — as well as fields beyond, including physicians, surgeons, and nutritionists. We've run hands-on clinical skill workshops like our suturing workshop, and we bring the community together through sports fundraisers like our recurring 3v3 basketball tournament.

Finding Your Path

We're working on connecting members with mentorship opportunities — check out our Mentorship page to meet e-board members available to chat. While we may not be able to provide research or shadowing opportunities directly as a club, we want to teach you how to find these opportunities — and through our growing network of guest speakers and members with space in their labs, you'll be well positioned to find shadowing and research through this community.
