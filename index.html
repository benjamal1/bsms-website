Brown Sports Medicine Society

The Brown Sports Medicine Society is Brown's only undergraduate orthopedics interest group, providing students with opportunities to break into orthopedic research, patient care, shadowing, and networking. We strive to create meaningful connections and conversations.

Interest Form

What We Do

We regularly host guest speakers from sports medicine, surgery, and orthopedics — as well as fields beyond, including physicians, surgeons, and nutritionists. We've run hands-on clinical skill workshops like our suturing workshop, and we bring the community together through sports fundraisers like our recurring 3v3 basketball tournament.

Bear Bones Newsletter

Bear Bones is the official sports medicine newsletter from the Brown Sports Medicine Society (BSMS). Our goal is to provide concise, evidence-based updates on athlete injuries, rehabilitation, and research for undergraduate and medical students interested in sports medicine and orthopedics.
